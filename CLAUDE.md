# Instructions for AI agents

Junction 2026, AstraZeneca challenge "Equal Care Across Finland". Read `README.md` first.

Judging: actionability 30 %, insight 25 %, data quality and transparency 20 %, execution 15 %, clarity 10 %.
Minimum requirements: public data with a visible source for every claim, at least one indicator of goal 3 of the
national cancer strategy (equal and effective care: survival, mortality, time to treatment), concrete recommendations,
a working demo.

- Every number on screen comes from `src/datastore.py` and carries its source (`source` dict). No number is typed into the UI or generated by a model.
- Arithmetic stays in code. A language model (Gemini) may explain and choose, never compute. Its text goes through `src/grounding.py`; unmatched numbers are flagged in the UI.
- A recommendation must cite a roadmap action (`src/strategy.py actions`, id + page). No citation, no recommendation.
- Mark assumptions with a comment `ASSUMPTION:` and show them in the UI as assumptions.
- Say what the data cannot show. Survival rates in Sotkanet have no case counts, so small counties (Åland) are noisy; screening attendance counts only the public programme (private mammography counts as absence).
- Run from the repo root: `make app`, `make web`, `make findings`, `make check`. Data is committed under `data/`; the demo never calls Sotkanet live. `data/raw/` is a cache and is not committed.
- Do not commit `.env`.

## Simulated residents (switchable engine)
- `src/population.py` builds Finland's 30-74 year olds as weighted cells from Statistics Finland tables; `src/residents.py` asks a decision engine once per archetype whether the person takes part in screening and why not; `src/simulate.py` anchors, aggregates and runs the what-if scenarios into `data/sim/*.json`.
- Engine switch: `SIM_ENGINE` in `.env` or environment (`jev` | `gemini`), or `--engine`. `jev` = Jev (TypeSafe), cache `data/jev/`, `TYPESAFE_API_KEY`; `gemini` = Gemini Decisions API (`gemini-decision-1`), cache `data/decisions/`, `GEMINI_API_KEY`. Prompt wording is shared; only the client call differs.
- Simulation is never shown as data. Every simulated number carries `note`/validation, and the UI labels it "Simulation" with the engine name.
- Report validation with every claim from the simulation (2024, real vs simulated attendance, Jev): breast r = 0.75 by county, colorectal 0.58, cervical 0.24 (weak: much cervical testing happens outside the programme). Use breast and colorectal for the story. Re-validate after switching to Gemini.
- The useful output is the split of a county's gap into what its population explains (`composition_gap`) and what it does not (`other_gap`), plus scenario effects in people per year.
- Answers are cached in `data/<engine cache>/` and committed, so the demo needs no key. Changing a state or question changes the cache hash and triggers new calls.
