Step 2 · Why does the gap exist? — the data half

7.3-point gap = 4.7 who lives there + 2.6 not explained

  • No AI needed for this split. Invited-weighted regression over 291 municipalities (R² = 60%) on real attendance.
  • 4.7 pts (90% CI 1.3–5.8) from population composition — language, education, age, municipality size.
  • 2.6 pts unexplained by composition — local access, services, or private mammography we can't see.

Step 2 · Why — the simulation half (the core)

771 representative residents, asked by two engines

  • Statistics Finland tables → 771 weighted resident archetypes for Helsinki (age × language × education × work). No invented people.
  • Gemini Decisions API answers one question per type: will she attend, what stops her? → probability + barrier. Jev answers the same — a range, not a point.
  • Validated: r = 0.84 vs real county rates. It overstates the language gap (60.8% real vs 48% sim) — shown in-app, not hidden.
  • Every dot on the map opens the exact prompt and recorded answer.

Step 3 · What would help?

Same residents, new answers — +992 to +2,093 women/yr

  • Re-ask the same archetypes under a counterfactual: invitations & outreach in her own language.
  • Two independent engines disagree → we report a range: Jev +992 · Gemini +2,093 = 45–95% of Helsinki's gap. Simulated upper bound, not a forecast.
  • Every intervention is matched to roadmap text and cited: Action 1.3.1, p. 33 (+1.1.1, p. 26) — procurement-ready, not a generic "raise awareness".

Step 4 · The rest of the pathway — Strategy Goal 3

Beyond screening: Satakunta waits 37.5% vs 3.5%

  • Five-stage pathway per county: detection → referral → diagnosis → treatment → outcome, from real THL queue data month by month.
  • Satakunta: 37.5% of oncology patients waited >90 days (Finland 3.5%) — shown with an explicit n = 8 · small numbers warning, not as a headline.
  • Gemini ranks which stage to fix first from county stats — a prioritisation aid, labelled as such.
  • Data gap exposed: stage at diagnosis is recorded for only ~42% of cancers nationally — itself Roadmap action 3.2.1.

Honest AI · built for decision-makers

Gemini reasons. Code counts.

  • LLMs never do arithmetic. Every figure comes from deterministic Python over THL Sotkanet, Statistics Finland, THL queues & the roadmap's 73 parsed actions.
  • Gemini's roles: Decisions per archetype, roadmap semantic matching, stage prioritisation, briefing/slides + illustration — and the TTS narration of our video.
  • Generated text is grounding-checked: numbers not in the data get flagged. Hosted demo replays recorded answers ("0 new calls"); reports are live within a budget.
  • Limits out loud: private mammography counts as absence; simulation ≠ observed data.
